Privacy Policy for Pour Over

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android puzzle game Pour Over (the “App”) handles information. For privacy questions, contact the developer at additivecd@gmail.com.

1. Game data stored on your device

Pour Over does not require you to register an account. The App saves game progress, preferences, sound and display settings, and related game state locally on your device using app storage. The App does not operate its own online account or game-progress server. You can generally remove this local information by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Clearing local data may reset game progress and settings.

2. Advertising and related information

The App integrates Google AdMob to provide interstitial and rewarded advertising. When the advertising SDK is active, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • device and app identifiers, including Android advertising identifiers when available and permitted;
  • advertising and app interaction information, such as ad views, taps, and app launches; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

These parties may use the information to serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, maintain security, and understand service performance. Ad personalization and the handling of identifiers depend on device settings, applicable law, and your consent choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for more information.

3. Advertising consent and choices

Where required, the App uses the advertising provider's consent flow to request advertising consent. You may also manage available advertising settings and reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Consent options and available controls may vary by country and device. If a consent choice needs to be changed after the initial request, contact us at the address above if the App does not provide a direct option.

4. In-app purchases

Pour Over includes an optional “Remove ads” purchase using Google Play Billing. Purchases and payment processing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Google Play and the purchase integration may process transaction or entitlement information needed to complete, restore, and verify a purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's handling of that data.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell locally stored game progress. Information may be handled by Google and its service providers in connection with AdMob advertising, consent management, Google Play Billing, service diagnostics, fraud prevention, and legal requirements. The App's locally saved game state is not intentionally transmitted to an independent game account server operated by us.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game settings and progress remain on your device until cleared, overwritten, or removed by uninstalling the App. Information processed by Google or its partners is retained under their respective policies. For questions or applicable privacy requests concerning data we control, contact us at the email address above. For Google-managed payment and advertising data, you may also use the relevant Google account and device privacy controls.

7. Security

We aim to minimize information handled directly by the App. Third-party services use their own security measures for information transmitted to them. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not require players to submit a name, email address, or create an account. Advertising and consent requirements may vary for children and families. If the App is directed to children or is enrolled in a families program, its advertising configuration and this policy must be reviewed for compliance with applicable children's privacy requirements before publication.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features, service providers, or legal obligations change. Any revisions will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
